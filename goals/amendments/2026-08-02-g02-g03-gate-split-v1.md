# G02/G03 Gate split amendment V1

## Decision

User authorization on 2026-08-02 requires the program to split G02 and start
G03 without weakening any unproved or production claim.

The G02 platform foundation development Gate is complete at commit
`16ab4af98c5f3b453ad3d9bdd1ae5fe959a37720`. Its final independent review has
no open Critical, High, Important or Minor finding. That Gate is sufficient to
start G03 because identity, durability, event, audit, receipt, billing,
bounded-work, clock, recovery and failure boundaries exist.

The distinct G02 production Evidence Gate remains `blocked_external`, with
`G02-E09-DEPENDENCY`, `G02-E12-LONG-MEDIA`, `G02-E14-REGION` and
`G02-E15-NATIVE` still `not_run`. This amendment does not promote any of them
and does not make G02 or G03 production eligible.

## Precedence and immutable inputs

This later user-authorized amendment supersedes only the G03 prerequisite text
`G02 completed` with `G02 platform_foundation_gate_completed`. It does not
rewrite the frozen G02 or G03 Goal files, their SHA-256 identities, the
canonical manifest snapshot, any Authority, technical acceptance requirement,
evidence status or non-claim.

Every other clause of
`goals/goal-03-sip-call-durable-foundation.md` remains binding. G03 must produce
its own current-state audit, contracts, wire corpus, TDD implementation,
evidence and independent review. Anything unproved remains `not_run`.

## Non-authorization

This amendment authorizes development entry only. It does not authorize
production or frozen-server mutation, inherited benchmark/evidence claims,
removal of rsipstack, a second Call Authority, or bypass of G03 acceptance
Gates.
